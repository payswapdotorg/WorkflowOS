# DEP-010 — Observability, Alerting, and Cost Controls

**Status:** PLANNED
**Program:** V2 productionization
**Parallelism:** PARALLEL_AFTER_DEPENDENCIES
**Dependencies:** DEP-002, DEP-006, DEP-008

## Objective
Provide production monitoring, alerting, tracing/logging, and provider cost controls sufficient to detect correctness and operational failures without creating new application authorities.

## Scope
Health/uptime monitors, API/Worker logs, traces/exception capture, deployment alerts, dependency outage alerts, provider usage thresholds, cost alerts, and incident links.

## Non-goals
Replacing application evidence/audit authorities with an observability provider.

## Required evidence
Known-failure alerts trigger; dependency failures are visible; cost thresholds are tested; sensitive data is excluded from telemetry; staging rehearsal; exact-head review.

## Downstream
DEP-011 and DEP-012.

## Completion
Architect merge after alert rehearsal and exact-head verification.
